After the golden path you have a verified wallet, a project, and an agent that has read the rules file. The cards below stop the hand-holding. Each one names a recipe, a network and a proof, and the shape is always the same: what to build, who may use it, where it runs, how to show it works. Once you have the shape, you will write your own prompts that way.

A card is verified only when its prompt has been executed on a fresh scaffold and the result compiles, passes the five credential-state tests and clears pre-flight; it then carries the date and the tools used. ## Who has already tried this network

One reason to build here is access to possible capital markets, and since "possible" is doing a lot of work in that sentence, here is what I know.

[Project Acacia's final report](https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/tokenised-money/pdf/project-acacia-final-report.pdf) was published by the Reserve Bank of Australia and the Digital Finance CRC on 18 May 2026, and I read it in full on 20 September 2026. It names Redbelly Network as one of three platforms on which the RBA issued pilot wholesale central bank digital currency, and it is the only one of the three the report calls public-permissioned. By my count from the report's Figure 1 and Appendix 1, four of its 12 pilots with real money and real assets ran on this network: tokenised corporate bonds, units in a wholesale fund, and a security backed by loans. The report's body calls one of those four a proof of concept.

I would not claim more from the report than that. It was a research project under temporary regulatory relief, and the pilot currency was redeemed when it ended. The report does not assess or endorse any platform. Nothing in it is in production. And nothing in it, or on this site, says that building here brings capital, investors or a listing.

For your code it means something narrower and more useful. The assets those pilots tokenised are the kind project 5 below builds: a bond that checks its own holders on every mint, transfer and claim. An issuer working under regulation will expect what the kit already enforces, which is a gate tested in all five credential states and every admin role behind a Safe and a timelock.

## The eighteen cards

Every card answers the same five things, in the same order: what it is, why it needs this network, who would transact on it and how often, what exists today, and where to start. The third is a description and never a forecast. A card that could not answer the second would not be here.

Pick whichever one you like the sound of. Every card opens its own project page, which takes you through it one step at a time, with a checklist, a track for directing an agent and a track for writing the Solidity yourself, and help with saving your work. Every project has been run and says what happened; 15 and 18 ask for a real network and their runs stopped at a fork, which their badges say. Project 17 read the real registers on mainnet, read-only, from a browser. Projects 13 and 14 are the ones the network's founder said out loud he would like someone to build, on the network's own podcast, and each card says which minute. Project 15 came from another builder's question in the network's own Discord in October 2026, and the answer they were given. Project 16 came from paying my own enable fee: the wallet said one figure, the chain said another, and the [What it costs](/fees/) page is the same checker running on this site. Projects 17 and 18 are the two that read and pay the ERC-3643 registers already on mainnet, which is where this network's real assets live today.
