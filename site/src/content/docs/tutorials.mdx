After the golden path you have a verified wallet, a project, and an agent that has read the rules file. The cards below stop the hand-holding. Each one names a recipe, a network and a proof, and the shape is always the same: what to build, who may use it, where it runs, how to show it works. Once you have the shape, you will write your own prompts that way.

Every prompt is executed before it is published, on a fresh scaffold, with at least two agents. It ships only if the result compiles, passes the five credential-state tests and clears pre-flight, and it carries the date and the tools used. CI re-runs the set monthly, and a failing card is pulled. <StatusPill variant="verified" label="Warm-ups run 12 Sep 2026, one agent" /> Cards 1 to 4 ran on 12 September 2026 with Claude Code as the agent; each card links to its run record (prompt, diff, `forge test`, pre-flight, wall time, tools). I reworded two of the four prompts because the run showed they did not fit the scaffold; the records say what changed and why. A second agent has not run them yet, and the monthly CI re-run is not wired. <StatusPill variant="pending" label="Cards 5 to 12 not run" /> Those still read "Verification pending", and that is what it means.

## The twelve cards

Pick whichever one you like the sound of. Every card opens its own project page, which takes you through it one step at a time, with a checklist, a track for directing an agent and a track for writing the Solidity yourself, and help with saving your work. Projects 1 to 4 have been run and say what happened. Projects 5 to 12 have not been run yet, and their pages say so plainly and give you only the parts that are proven.
