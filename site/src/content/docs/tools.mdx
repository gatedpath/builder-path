## What the contract kit gives you

Every mint, burn and transfer of the kit's `GatedERC20` ends in this function: the wallet losing tokens and the wallet gaining them must both be eligible, never a spender, and only a compliance officer's forced transfer skips the check on the sender.

<Sample id="kit-update" />

The forced transfer itself requires a role and an eligible recipient, and it puts the hash of the document that authorised it on the log for good.

<Sample id="kit-force-transfer" />

## What the frontend kit gives you

There is one hook. It reads the network's gate and the dApp's gate, caches both, refetches on a new block or every thirty seconds, and gives the page one of seven states to render.
