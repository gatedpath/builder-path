## Right now

<LiveStatus />

What the cards read and how. `eth_chainId` and `eth_blockNumber` say the RPC is up and on the chain it should be; the latency is one round trip for a batch of five calls from wherever you are. The block interval is computed the way `phase-0/measure-blocktime.mjs` computes it, from the timestamps of consecutive blocks over the last 25, so it changes with traffic: blocks on Redbelly are produced on demand, and a quiet quarter of an hour gives one long interval, not a slow chain. The base fee comes from the latest block, the RBNT price from the on-chain feed with its own timestamp, and the transfer cost is the two multiplied; Vine prices a native transfer at US$0.01 and the figure should sit there whatever RBNT is worth. Every call goes from your browser to the RPC and nowhere else. When an RPC does not answer, its card says so and keeps the figures from the build, dated.

<VineAside href={LINKS.vineEnvironments} label="Environments on Vine">
The RPC URLs, chain IDs and explorers are Redbelly's; the cards read them from `@gatedpath/chains`, which recorded them from this page.
</VineAside>

### Gas price at the time of the Phase 0 pass

At 08:25 UTC on 12 September 2026 the mainnet price feed returned 2427, which is US$0.002427 per RBNT at the feed's six decimals; the base fee was 196,205,387,799,948 wei, so a 21,000-gas transfer cost 4.12 RBNT, US$0.01. The priority fee was zero. Source: the research log, question 6. The live card above reads the same two values now.

<Aside type="note" title="Where the numbers come from">
The live cards: your browser, the public governors RPCs, nothing in between; the code is `site/src/lib/status-reader.ts` and runs once at build too. The tables: `report/data/metrics.json`, generated {ts(metrics.generatedAt)} by `report/scripts/build.mjs` from RPC reads and public Routescan API reads. Nothing here uses a wallet, a key or a transaction. The site's build copies the file and fails if the copy drifts from the source.
</Aside>
