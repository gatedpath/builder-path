Vine explains the network, and these pages do not repeat it. Each one links to the page that does, states what I measured against the RPC on 12 September 2026, and says what that means for the code you write. A page that would still make sense with Vine gone is theirs, and I have cut it.

- [Consensus and finality](/concepts/consensus-and-finality/): leaderless DBFT with deterministic finality, and the reorg-handling code you delete.
- [Blocks on demand](/concepts/blocks-on-demand/): there is no block time; there is either traffic or there is not.
- [Gas model](/concepts/gas-model/): fees priced in US dollars, paid in RBNT, with a zero priority fee.
- [Identity gate](/concepts/identity-gate/): every wallet passes `isAllowed` before it can send, and what that does to your threat model.

The four research papers these rest on are summarised on the [research page](/research/).
