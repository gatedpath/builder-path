Every entry below is generated at build from `packages/agent-rules/src/failures.ts`. The MCP server's `explain_failure` and the frontend kit's `explainError` read that same file, so the words an agent sees and the words a page shows are the words here. Each entry says what happened, why it happened, and then the next command or the page to read. Where a fact belongs to the network, the entry links to Vine or docs.redbelly.network and adds only the consequence for your code.

One entry is someone else's observation rather than ours: the error the real network returns to a wallet that has no write access yet. A fork and a local Anvil do not enforce the node-level permission map, and nothing of ours has signed against 151 or 153 yet, so I have not seen it. A DAO developer, Rahman, published the wording on 9 July 2026 with a testnet transaction as evidence: `Sender not authorised to write transactions`. The tools now recognise it, the entry says whose observation it is and links to his write-up, and it moves to measured the day one of us captures it.
